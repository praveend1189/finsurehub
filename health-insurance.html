Benefits

Benefits of health insurance

These are the benefits families usually ask us to look for. Exact cover, waiting periods and network hospitals vary by plan.

Cashless treatment facility

Use the insurer’s network hospitals so you may not need to pay the full bill upfront.

Treatment in large hospitals

Access a wide hospital network so you can choose a facility that fits the treatment you need.

No capping on room rent

Ask about plans that do not restrict room rent to a daily sub-limit.

No capping on all critical illnesses

Look for critical-illness cover without a separate inner cap, where the plan allows it.

Security for the whole family

Individual and family-floater options so parents and children can sit on one discussion.

Tax-saving benefit (Section 80D)

Premiums on eligible health plans may qualify for a deduction under Section 80D. Your tax advisor can confirm what applies to you.

Policy features

Characteristics of our policy

We walk through these features when we compare plans. Nothing here is a guarantee of every policy.

No limit on room rent (no capping)

Plans that avoid a daily room-rent cap, where the insurer offers that option.

All critical illness cover (no capping)

Discuss listed critical illnesses and whether a sub-limit applies.

Pre & post hospitalisation cover

Many plans cover related tests and medicines before and after a hospital stay.

AYUSH treatment cover

Ask whether Ayurveda, Yoga, Unani, Siddha or Homeopathy treatment is included.

Ambulance charges cover

Emergency ambulance costs are often part of the hospitalisation benefit, up to plan limits.

Recharge of sum insured option

Some plans restore the sum insured after a claim in the same year. We will show you if it applies.
